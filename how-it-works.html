How QuickMail Works

Create a temporary email address and receive messages without using your personal email address.

1. Create a Temporary Email

Open QuickMail and a temporary email address will be generated for you automatically.

2. Copy Your Email Address

Use the Copy button to copy your temporary email address. You can then use it when a website requires an email address.

3. Receive Messages

Messages sent to your temporary email address will appear in your QuickMail inbox.

4. Open and Read Messages

Click a message in your inbox to view the sender, subject, date, and message content.

5. Create a New Email

When you need another temporary address, simply click the New Email button to create a new inbox.
