ABOUT QUICKMAIL

Simple Temporary Email

QuickMail provides temporary email addresses that can be used when you do not want to share your personal email address.

What is QuickMail?

QuickMail is a simple temporary email service that allows users to create a temporary email address quickly. Messages sent to the temporary address can be viewed through the QuickMail inbox.

Why use a temporary email?

A temporary email can be useful when signing up for websites, testing online services, or avoiding unnecessary messages in your personal inbox.

Our goal

Our goal is to provide a simple, fast, and easy-to-use temporary email experience with a clean interface.
