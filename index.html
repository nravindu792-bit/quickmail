ABOUT TEMPORARY EMAIL

What is a Temporary Email?

A temporary email is a disposable email address that can be used when you do not want to share your personal email address. It can help reduce unwanted emails and keep your main inbox more organized.

Protect Your Inbox

Use a temporary address for websites and services that you do not want connected to your personal inbox.
