Your Privacy Matters

This Privacy Policy explains how QuickMail handles information when you use our website.

1. Information We Handle

QuickMail creates temporary email addresses for users. Information related to temporary inboxes and received messages may be processed to provide the service.

2. Temporary Email Messages

Temporary email addresses are designed for short-term use. Users should not use QuickMail for sensitive, confidential, or important personal information.

3. Cookies

QuickMail may use cookies or similar technologies to improve website functionality, understand website usage, and support advertising services.

4. Advertising

QuickMail may use third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense. These services may use cookies or similar technologies to provide and measure advertisements.

Users may be able to control personalized advertising through Google's advertising settings.

5. Third-Party Services

QuickMail may use third-party services to provide temporary email functionality, hosting, analytics, security, or advertising.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technologies, or legal requirements change.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the QuickMail website administrator.
