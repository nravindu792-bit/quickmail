Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about QuickMail and temporary email.

What is a temporary email?

A temporary email is an email address designed for short-term use. It can help you receive messages without sharing your personal email address.

Is QuickMail free to use?

QuickMail is designed to provide temporary email functionality without requiring users to create a traditional email account.

How do I get a temporary email address?

Open the QuickMail homepage. A temporary email address will be generated automatically for you.

Can I receive emails?

Yes. Emails sent to your temporary address can appear in your QuickMail inbox.

Can I create another email address?

Yes. You can use the New Email button to generate another temporary email address.

How do I read a received email?

When a message appears in your inbox, click the message to open it and view its contents.

Are temporary emails permanent?

No. Temporary email addresses and messages are intended for temporary use and may not remain available permanently.

Should I use temporary email for important accounts?

Temporary email is generally intended for short-term situations. For important accounts, recovery addresses, or important communications, use a permanent email address that you control.

Can I use QuickMail for unlawful activities?

No. QuickMail must not be used for unlawful activities, fraud, abuse, spam, harassment, or other prohibited activities.

How can I contact QuickMail?

For questions about QuickMail, please use the contact information provided on our Contact page.
